## 1. Master's Dissertation - Cancer Psycho-Oncology

### Coping Styles and Psychological Distress in Palliative Cancer Patients

Conducted a quantitative exploratory correlational study examining the relationship between coping styles and psychological distress among **80 palliative cancer patients** receiving treatment at a tertiary cancer-care hospital.

The study examined four coping domains-**approach, avoidance, altered-consciousness, and support-seeking coping**-and their associations with **anxiety and depression**.

### Methods
- Brief COPE Inventory
- Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS)
- Quantitative exploratory correlational design
- Purposive sampling
- Interviewer-administered assessment
- SPSS
- Descriptive statistics
- Pearson correlation
- Multiple linear regression
- Regression assumption and multicollinearity diagnostics

### Key finding
Approach-oriented coping showed significant inverse associations with both anxiety and depression. In multiple regression models, coping domains accounted for **19.9% of the variance in anxiety** and **54.3% of the variance in depression**, with approach coping showing the strongest unique association with both outcomes.

The manuscript has been **submitted to a peer-reviewed psychology journal and is currently under editorial/review consideration**.

## 2. Human Figure Drawing Test (HFDT) Research

### The Persistence of the Personal Fable in Emerging Adulthood

Co-authored research project conducted with **Kaushlendra Mani Tripathi, PhD**, examining how **Elkind's Personal Fable**=omnipotence, invulnerability, and uniqueness-may be expressed through visual self-representation in emerging adulthood.

The study involved **30 first-year undergraduate students aged 18–19 years** and used the **Human Figure Drawing Test (HFDT)** together with participants' brief reflective narratives.

### Methodology
- Human Figure Drawing Test (HFDT)
- Qualitative exploratory and interpretative design
- Whole–Part–Whole analytic logic
- Visual thematic analysis
- Narrative analysis
- Inductive micro-coding
- Cross-case and cross-gender synthesis
- Theoretical mapping of HFDT indicators to Personal Fable domains

My contributions included **methodology, data collection, formal analysis, interpretation, and manuscript writing**, alongside the conceptual and supervisory contributions of the co-author.

The manuscript is **currently undergoing ethical review** and is not yet presented as a published study.
