## Current Research Direction

My current doctoral research interests focus on psychological and cognitive functioning in adults with neurological illness, particularly the relationship between **attachment, coping, psychological distress, and cognitive functioning** in adults with primary brain tumors.

A preliminary conceptual framework is:

**Attachment insecurity → Coping → Psychological distress → Cognitive functioning**

with particular interest in the distinction between **subjective cognitive complaints and objectively assessed neuropsychological performance**.
